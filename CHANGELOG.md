# Workspaces changelog

What changed between versions, in the words you'd notice it. `RECIPE.md`
describes the whole thing as it is now; this file says what's new since the
version you have (`mods/ws-switcher/VERSION` in your copy). To update, see
"Updating" in the README.

## [Unreleased]

### New
- **Right-click an agent.** In the Agents list, right-clicking an agent's row (a sub-agent's, or a number on the narrow rail) opens a menu: Peek; Send a message…, a box that types into its prompt and sends it without going there; Interrupt while it works (Esc); Compact while it rests (`/compact`, Claude Code, Codex and Pi); Fork session, a new tab beside it that starts a copy of the conversation with the same preset (`--resume <id> --fork-session` for Claude, `--fork` for Pi); Rename…; Pin to top; Copy terminal id (its Herdr address, such as `t7`, for `hc read` and `hc send`); Copy session id; Close session, which asks first while it works or waits on you; and Wipe session…, which after a confirmation closes it and moves its conversation (Claude Code's transcript and the folders Claude keeps for it, or Pi's session file) to the Trash, so History and resume no longer find it. Items show only where they work: Codex has no Fork, Wipe or Copy session id, Antigravity no Fork or Wipe, and an agent that shows no numbers on its row has none of the three. To update, copy `mods/ws-agents`.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-21

### New
- **Each agent's model, context and compactions, and your Claude and Antigravity usage.** In the Agents list, a Claude Code, Pi or Antigravity agent's row has a second line: its model, how full its context is (a small bar and the percent, or the tokens where the window's size isn't known) and how many times it was compacted. Hovering that line says the rest: the effort or thinking level, the tokens against the window, the last compaction (automatic or `/compact`, and how big it was), and for Claude and Pi what the session has cost. The status bar at the bottom shows how much of your Claude and Antigravity plans you've used: each plan's icon and its most used limit. A click opens a panel like the one in Claude's own app, with every limit (for Claude the week, a model's own week such as Fable's, and the 5-hour window when your plan reports one; for Antigravity the weekly and 5-hour limits of its Gemini, Claude and GPT, and Argon groups), when each resets, and a Refresh. Every 10 minutes it asks each agent's own `/usage` headless (`claude -p /usage`, with your hooks and MCP servers off and no session kept; `agy -p /usage`, with no conversation kept and its log sent to `/dev/null`), which reads the numbers with that agent's own sign-in and costs nothing; until Claude's answers, it shows the limits Claude's status line last reported. The status bar is a new copy of Herdr's, `ws-status`, so more agents' plans can join later. Claude started through the launcher in a Herdr terminal gets a status line, `herdr-ws-statusline`, that keeps what Claude hands it and shows nothing; a status line you set yourself wins, so put `herdr-ws-statusline` in front of your own command to have both. A Claude started some other way still shows its model, tokens and compactions, read from its transcript. Pi started through the launcher loads a small extension (`mods/ws-switcher/pi/herdr-stats.ts`) that keeps Pi's own numbers the same way, and Antigravity's come from its transcript after its first prompt. A Node script reads them, as History's does. To update, copy `layouts/workspaces.json`, `mods/ws-agents`, `mods/ws-switcher` and the new `mods/ws-status`; to feed the list from your own status line, link `herdr-ws-statusline` into `~/.local/bin` beside `herdr-ws-agent`. Agents started before the update show what their transcript says (Claude) or nothing (Pi) until you start them again.
- **History shows compactions.** A Claude Code, Pi or Antigravity conversation in History shows how many times it was compacted, beside its model; opened, it lists when each compaction was (newest first), whether it was automatic or `/compact`, the context's size before and after, and what was in the context at the end. It comes from the transcripts on your computer, so it costs nothing but reading them: the first time History opens after the update it reads each transcript of the last 90 days in full once (a couple of seconds), then only what one gained. Codex, Grok and OpenCode don't record their compactions where History reads. To update, copy `mods/ws-history`.
- **A foreman for Claude's safety stops.** A long Claude reply can be cut by the safety classifier, which ends that helper's turn mid-task. The new `foreman` preset avoids most of it by never handing out a task whole: it runs on Pi with a non-Claude model, splits the work into one- and two-call steps, gives each to a fresh `hand` helper, and reads every stop. It resumes with the smallest missing step, halves the step if that is stopped too, answers Claude's "switch to Opus" offer, and after a third stop does the step itself. `hand` is the worker it starts: Sonnet at low effort, built for a reply of a line or two; `hand-strong` is the same on Opus at high effort, for a step Sonnet got wrong. The starter guide now sends an agent to `foreman` where a helper's long replies keep being cut. To update, copy `examples/presets/foreman.md`, `hand.md` and `hand-strong.md` to `~/.config/herdr-core/templates/`, copy `mods/ws-switcher`, and add the guide's new sentence to your own `~/.herdr/AGENTS.md` if you wrote one. The foreman's command names the uncensored model `deepseek-v4.1-flash-uncensored` through Pi, so change that line in Agent presets if your Pi has another non-Claude model.

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-20

### New
- **Antigravity in History, with model choices, and back after a restart.** History lists Antigravity's conversations beside the other agents' (the ones you talked to, not `agy -p` runs), in the folder they ran in, and Resume continues one with `--conversation`. The Presets and Automations screens offer its models (the families `agy models` lists, such as `gemini-4-neon`, asked once a day) and its effort levels. And an Antigravity agent started through the launcher comes back after a reboot or a logout like Claude does: the launcher puts a hook in its instructions folder that notes the conversation, and `herdr-ws-resume` continues it. One difference: Antigravity has no hook for its end, so one you left in a tab you kept open comes back too (`herdr-ws-resume --forget` in that tab stops it). To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher`, `mods/ws-history`, `mods/ws-presets` and `mods/ws-automations`; Antigravity agents started before the update come back only after you start them again.
- **Pi gets the workspace's instructions too.** Pi started through the launcher now gets what Claude, Codex and Antigravity get: your global guide, the workspace's `AGENTS.md`, the brief and a preset's instructions, as system instructions (`--append-system-prompt`). So a Pi session in Herdr knows about automations, the board and `hc`, starts your presets as helpers and reports back with `hc send`. A preset whose command runs `pi` now gets its instructions that way too, with its task as the first message, from the + menu, the board, an automation or `hc run … herdr-ws-agent --preset`. Pi has no `--add-dir`, so its instructions name the workspace's other folders and its `tasks` folder instead; Pi has no sandbox either, so nothing keeps it to them. Pi already reads `AGENTS.md` from its folder and the folders above it, so a file it reads that way (the guide, for Pi started in a Herdr worktree or the Scratch root) isn't sent twice. Your own `~/.pi/agent/APPEND_SYSTEM.md` still applies; a folder's `.pi/APPEND_SYSTEM.md` is only pointed at (Watch out in the recipe says why). To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher`, `mods/ws-board` and `mods/ws-presets`, and, if you have Pi, add `"pi": { "run": ["herdr-ws-agent", "pi"] }` to the programs in `programs.json` (only then: through the launcher Herdr would list it as installed either way). The starter guide's opening comment now names Pi; change yours in `~/.herdr/AGENTS.md` if you want the same.

## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-20

### New
- **Scratch is a group of its scratch spaces, with its own +.** In the switcher menu, Scratch is now a header at the bottom with your open scratch spaces under it, then the scratch folders kept with files (a click opens one again), so they're one click away from any workspace; it folds like a group. "New scratch space…" is gone from the top of the menu: the + on the Scratch header asks for a name and makes one (the Spaces + while Scratch is shown, and the command `ws-switcher.scratch-new`, still do too). To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher`.
- **Groups and your own order in the switcher menu.** With many workspaces the menu was one long alphabetical list. Now a workspace can have a group (`"group": "Work"` in its `workspace.jsonc`, or right-click it in the menu, or "Put … in a group…"), and a group's workspaces sit under a header that folds with a click; a folded group still shows the workspace you're in, and the dot when one of its agents needs you. Drag any row to put it where you want it: a workspace onto another goes above or below it and into its group, onto a header goes into that group, and a header moves its whole group. Right-click a header to rename the group or ungroup it. The order and folded groups are kept in `~/.herdr/workspaces/.order.json`; Scratch stays last. To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher` (it has a new file, `arrange.js`).
- **A scout, a researcher and a planner.** Three more example presets, for what an agent would otherwise hand to a subagent of its own, where you can't watch it or type to it. `scout` finds where something is and how it works in the code and answers with files and lines. `researcher` answers what the repo can't, from docs, source and the web, with a source for each finding and what it couldn't check. `planner` reads the code and writes a plan an implementer can follow. None of them changes code, and they run in Claude's auto mode, so a helper never waits for someone to answer a prompt. The starter guide (`mods/ws-switcher/guide.md`) now tells Claude and Codex to start one of these where they'd use their own subagent. To update, copy `examples/presets/scout.md`, `researcher.md` and `planner.md` to `~/.config/herdr-core/templates/` and copy `mods/ws-switcher`. If you wrote your own `~/.herdr/AGENTS.md`, add the starter's new first line under "Helper agents: hc".
- **Antigravity gets the workspace's instructions too.** Antigravity's CLI (`agy`) started through the launcher now gets what Claude and Codex get: your global guide, the workspace's `AGENTS.md`, the brief and a preset's instructions. So an Antigravity session in Herdr knows about automations, the board and `hc`, and starts your presets as helpers. Antigravity has no flag for instructions, so the launcher writes them as a rules file in a folder of its own under `~/.herdr/agy/` and gives Antigravity that folder; nothing goes into your repo or into Antigravity's settings, and folders nobody has used for 90 days are removed. Three things to know. Antigravity asks before its first `hc send` to another session: choose "always allow for commands that start with 'hc'" and a helper reports back by itself after that. It asks before reading a file outside the workspace's folders. And all of this rests on how agy 1.5.13 behaves, which its docs don't promise (Watch out in the recipe says how to check after an update). To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher`, `mods/ws-board` and `mods/ws-presets`, and, if you have Antigravity, add `"antigravity": { "run": ["herdr-ws-agent", "agy"] }` to the programs in `programs.json` (only then: through the launcher Herdr would list it as installed either way). The starter guide now names Antigravity's own subagents beside Claude's and Codex's; add the two words to your `~/.herdr/AGENTS.md` if you want the same.

### Fixed
- **A helper no longer waits at "do you trust this folder?" for a repo it only reads.** The guide's line for starting a helper had `--cwd <repo>`, and Claude asks once about every repo it hasn't been started in, so a helper sent to read one sat at that question until you noticed. The starter guide and the line under `herdr-ws-agent --presets` now start a helper in the folder of the agent that starts it, with the repo named in its task, and keep `--cwd` for a helper that changes files somewhere else. To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher`, and in your own `~/.herdr/AGENTS.md` replace the line about `herdr-ws-agent --presets` with the starter's.

## [0.8.1] - 2026-11-20

### Fixed
- **Claude no longer asks about the cards folder at every start.** Since Claude Code 2.1.326, Claude started in a workspace that has cards stopped at "Adding a new directory: …/tasks" with "No, exit" picked, every time, because the launcher gave it that folder with `--add-dir`; an agent started by a card or an automation waited there. Claude now gets the workspace's `tasks` folder, and the Scratch root, through its settings (`permissions.additionalDirectories`, merged into the command's own inline `--settings`), which lets it read and edit there without the question. A workspace root or outside folder Claude hasn't been told to trust still asks: start Claude there once and say yes. Agents already running keep the answer you gave them. To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher`.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-20

### New
- **History: every agent's past conversations, with Resume.** A History row in the sidebar footer (also "Resume a conversation…" in the + menu, and the command `ws-switcher.history`) lists the conversations your agents saved, newest first, for the workspace shown or everywhere: Claude Code, Codex, Pi, Grok and OpenCode together, with a chip per agent and search. **Resume** continues one in a new tab in the folder it ran in; one that's open in a terminal says so and offers **Go to** instead. It's the workspace's screen like the board, pinned as a pill; with All spaces it's a dialog. A Claude conversation started from a preset from now on resumes as that preset (its model, permissions and instructions). It only reads the agents' own stores. It needs Node 20.6 or newer (22.13, or the `sqlite3` program, for Codex and OpenCode). To update, copy the new `mods/ws-history` folder, copy `layouts/workspaces.json` again (it adds a `screen-history` block to `screens-body`, and the footer block is 153px), and copy `mods/ws-switcher`, `mods/ws-tabs`, `mods/ws-chrome` and `mods/ws-new-menu`.
- **A scratch space is named when you make it.** New scratch space (the switcher menu, the Spaces + while Scratch is shown, the empty Scratch's button) now asks for a name first instead of making "Scratch 10:20" at once, and the name goes into the folder too: "Bot idea" is a space called that in `~/.herdr/scratch/<date-time>-bot-idea`, so a folder that outlives its space still says what it was for. The folder lists show a named folder by its name, without the date. Enter on a blank name makes "Scratch HH:MM" as before. The command `ws-switcher.scratch-new` opens the same dialog; a script makes one at once with `ws-switcher.scratch-new/<name>`. To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher` and `mods/ws-spaces`.
- **The board and the worktrees belong to the workspace, not to a space.** Opening either shows it in place of the tabs from whichever space you're in, and pins it as a pill before the tabs of every space of the workspace. A click on a tab, a space or an agent goes back to the terminals and the pill stays; its × closes it for the workspace. They open without making a tab or a space, so also in a workspace with no spaces. A Board or Worktrees tab you have open becomes the pill by itself. `ws-switcher.board` and `ws-switcher.worktrees` are commands a key in `keys.json` can run. The board's view, folds and GitHub filters are now remembered per workspace and start from the defaults. To update, copy `layouts/workspaces.json` (it adds a `screens` column to the `center` stack) and `mods/ws-switcher`, `mods/ws-tabs`, `mods/ws-board`, `mods/ws-worktrees`, `mods/ws-spaces` and `mods/ws-agents`. With the mods but an older layout, both still open as tabs.

### Fixed
- **The Files and Changes panel no longer covers the terminals.** A panel widened in a big window kept that width in a smaller one, where it filled the whole work area and the terminal was gone: Herdr keeps a panel's width through any window size. The terminals now keep at least 400px. Dragging the divider stops there, and a panel wider than the window allows is narrowed to what fits, open or closed; it grows back to the width it had when the window has room again, unless you've dragged it since. To update, copy `layouts/workspaces.json` (its `main` block gets `"min": 400`) and `mods/ws-tabs` (it gains `fit.js`).
- **A card whose work began without Start no longer sits in Todo.** When a pull request opens on a card that's still in a column before the board's start one, the card moves to the finish column (Review), or to the start one (Doing) for a draft, with a toast. That covers work an orchestrator or you did without pressing the card's Start, and a taken card that picked up your pull request. It happens once per pull request, so a card you drag back stays. After updating, each open pull request already on such a card moves it once. The starter guide (`mods/ws-switcher/guide.md`) now also asks agents to set a card's `status:` when its work starts and when it's done, however the work reached them. To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher`, and add that line to your own `~/.herdr/AGENTS.md` if you wrote one (it's the first under "The board and the brief" in the starter).

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-18

### New
- **Your GitHub project board, in the board tab.** Tasks | GitHub in a board's header shows the workspace's GitHub project board: its own status columns, or a list with a section per status, with Everyone / Mine / Unassigned, a repo filter, search, and Newest or Oldest in place of the board's own order. **Take** on any item, yours or not, makes a card on your board with the issue's title, text, folder and priority. A taken card then shows the item's status on GitHub, follows a changed title, moves to your last column when the issue closes, and picks up a pull request of yours that closes it. Link a board by pasting its link in the view, or pick one it finds; it's saved as `"board": { "github": "<link>" }` in `workspace.jsonc`. A read is kept for 10 minutes (in `~/.herdr/cache/github-boards/`), so going back to the view, in any board tab or after a restart, shows the board at once without reading GitHub again; **Refresh** reads it now. It only reads: nothing changes on GitHub, so assign yourself there when your team expects it. It needs `gh` with the `read:project` scope (`gh auth refresh -s read:project`). To update, copy `mods/ws-board` and `mods/ws-switcher`.
- **A guide for every agent.** Write what every agent should know about Herdr (how to set up automations, start hc helpers, keep a card's notes) in `~/.herdr/AGENTS.md`, and Claude and Codex started through the launcher get it first, before the workspace's instructions: from the + menu, a preset, a card, an automation or `hc run`. It replaces the built-in line about `herdr-ws-run --add`. In Agent presets, under "Herdr guide", **Write one** starts it from a starter and **Edit** opens it; the switch beside them turns it off for every agent (on by default; `herdr-ws-agent --guide off` too), and a preset's **Goes without** (`guide: off`) leaves it out for that preset alone. The example `watcher` preset goes without it. To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher` and `mods/ws-presets`, and add `guide: off` to your copy of `watcher`.
- **The README shows each screen.** It's now a section per feature, with drawings of the task board, the GitHub view, the Worktrees tab and the Automations screen (`previews/`, from a `preview.svg` beside each mod). Nothing to do in an install.

### Fixed
- **An empty workspace no longer shows another project's terminal.** Closing a workspace's last space (exiting its agent, then its shell) kept the workspace chosen but showed a space from another workspace beside it, tabs and all; choosing a workspace with no spaces did the same. The work area is now empty instead, with "No spaces in <workspace>" and a New space button, until you show a space or choose another workspace. To update, copy `layouts/workspaces.json` (it wraps the work column in a `center` stack beside an `empty` block) and `mods/ws-switcher`.
- **Picking Scratch in the switcher menu shows it.** A click on the Scratch workspace did nothing: its row and New scratch space shared an id inside the menu, so the click got lost. Picking it from All spaces still worked. Every workspace row now has an id of its own, which also covers a workspace you call new, all, edit, move, rename or delete. To update, copy `mods/ws-switcher`.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-18

### New
- **Notifications reach your desktop.** While Herdr isn't the app in front, every toast (an agent needs you or finished, an automation's news, a pull request's) also shows as a system notification with the same words, and a click on it brings Herdr forward and goes where the toast would. With Keep running on and Herdr closed, the background runner's news goes to the desktop too, with the system's sound. `herdr-ws-notify --off` turns them off. To update: copy the new `mods/ws-toast` folder, copy `layouts/workspaces.json` again (or add `"toast": "ws-toast"` at its root), and link `bin/herdr-ws-notify` into `~/.local/bin` like the other scripts. - **A brief for each workspace.** `~/.herdr/workspaces/<id>/BRIEF.md` says where the workspace's work stands today, and whatever knows keeps it: an automation, a script, you. Claude and Codex started there with no preset get it after the workspace's instructions; a preset's agent is told where the file is. Nothing to do by hand: without the file nothing changes.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

The first published version, built for Herdr 0.1.14.

### New
- **Workspaces:** a switcher above the spaces list; the spaces and agents lists show only the chosen workspace. Each workspace has its own agent instructions, which Claude and Codex get from the + menu.
- **A task board** per workspace: one markdown file per task, cards that start an agent in the repo or a fresh worktree and show its live status.
- **Board cards follow their pull request:** checks, reviews, conflicts and merges show on the card, and the card's agent is told when a check fails or a review arrives.
- **Agent presets,** global or per workspace, started from the tab bar's + menu or a card's Start; an orchestrator preset that splits a task across helper agents.
- **Automations:** on a schedule (once or repeating), a board column, an agent finishing or needing you, file changes, a GitHub PR or issue, or a call from another app. Describe one in words and Claude drafts it. Quiet checks skip runs when there's nothing to do, and run only once you've allowed them.
- **Missed schedules** catch up when Herdr opens if they're under 12 hours old; older ones are reported.
- **Claude agents come back after a restart,** resumed with their conversations (a switch at the foot of the Agent presets screen).
- **A click on a sub-agent opens it as a tab.**
