# Workspaces changelog

What changed between versions, in the words you'd notice it. `RECIPE.md`
describes the whole thing as it is now; this file says what's new since the
version you have (`mods/ws-switcher/VERSION` in your copy). To update, see
"Updating" in the README.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.34.1] - 2026-11-27

### Fixed
- **Terminal file links with `#L` anchors or line ranges jump to their line in Files.** `ws-files` now uses Herdr 0.1.18's `link.line` / `link.column` and parses `#L42`, `#L5-L12` and `:5-12` suffixes (including on remote terminals).
- **Worktree removal checks locks and waits for terminals to exit.** Removing a worktree from the Worktrees screen now checks whether it is locked before closing its spaces, waits up to 10 seconds for closed space terminals to stop before running `git worktree remove`, and supports `<project>/.bare` repos (matching Herdr 0.1.17–0.1.18).
- **Board worktrees follow `worktrees.directory`.** Starting a card in a worktree reads `worktrees.directory` from Herdr's `settings.json` (instead of hardcoding `~/.herdr/worktrees`) and names `<project>/.bare` repos after the project.
- **Smaller Herdr 0.1.17–0.1.18 alignments.** Keeping a sub-agent from the Agents list picks a space on that sub-agent's own machine (falling back to peek if none exists); `agent.select` / `pane.focus` onto another pane in the same tab leaves a full-size view; `ws-spaces` guards `window()?.active` when no window is open.

## [0.34.0] - 2026-11-27

### New
- **Workspaces in the command palette.** ⌘⇧P lists, under Workspaces, Switch workspace…, New scratch space…, Show Board, Notes, Worktrees, History, Changes and Files, Views…, Notifications, and the package's Settings sections ("Workspaces settings: Features" and the rest). A view whose add-on is off in Settings > Features says so in a toast ("Board is off"), and a click on it opens Features. With the sidebar hidden since Herdr started, what needs the switcher says "Show the sidebar to do this", and a click shows it.
- **New space in an empty workspace.** A workspace with no spaces has a "New space in Acme" button under its message ("New scratch space…" in Scratch).
- **New space in a folder… in the + menu.** Under New space in the tab bar's + menu, it opens the New space dialog (a name and a folder), as holding the Spaces + does. Not in Scratch.
- **A workspace's own menu.** Right-click a workspace in the switcher menu, or the Scratch header, for Rename, Edit its settings, its group and Delete, for that workspace without showing it first. A right-click used to open only its group.
- **Arrow keys in the switcher menu.** ↑ ↓ move one lit row through the menu (it opens on the workspace you're in) and Enter opens it; the number keys show beside All spaces (0) and the workspace rows (1 to 9) and count the rows as the menu draws them, where they counted the workspaces in another order and showed nowhere.
- **Keep a scratch folder from its row.** Hovering a scratch folder in the switcher menu shows Keep, or Don't keep for a kept one, and the row's words change at once. Its right-click has Open, Keep or Don't keep, and Move to Trash…, which asks first and won't move a folder a terminal is in.
- **Errors stay in the bell.** An error toast goes by in a few seconds, cut to a line; the bell keeps each one as an update with its whole text, so a missed one can be read later. One an agent's or a run's own row already says isn't repeated. The line under the bell's title counts the red ones apart: "2 failed · 1 unread".
- **Close a row in the bell, or a toast.** Every row of the bell's list has a quiet × after its time, and Backspace or Delete closes the row picked with ↑ ↓, one per press. An update or a failure goes; an agent waiting on you, a check nobody allowed or a failing pull request is hidden until that's over and is back the next time. A toast has a × too, which closes it without doing what a click on it does, and its text runs to two lines.
- **Settings from the bell.** A gear in the bell's header, before Mark all read, opens Settings on Features, where the bell is switched on and off. With the sidebar hidden since Herdr started it says "Show the sidebar to do this" and the list stays open.
- **⋯ on agent rows.** Hovering an agent or a helper in the Agents list shows ⋯ (after an agent's pin); a click on it opens the agent's menu just under it, the same menu a right-click opens.
- **Close and Wipe count the helpers.** Closing or wiping an agent with live helpers asks first and says how many end with it: "It has 3 helpers (2 working): they end with it."
- **Send a message takes several lines.** Enter is a new line and ⌘Enter (Ctrl+Enter elsewhere) sends.
- **Agent presets and Automations: ⌘S saves, and a Close ×.** Ctrl+S elsewhere; "⌘S" sits beside Save (and Automations' Create). The × replaces the word "Esc" in Automations' header.
- **Discard beside Save in Automations and Secrets.** While there are unsaved edits, Discard asks once ("Discard changes?" in the button) and a second press puts back what was saved; a new automation or secret has Cancel there. Files' Discard beside Save asks the same way, where one press dropped every edit since the last save. In Secrets, Enter in a box while Discard or Delete asks only takes the question back.
- **Rename… and Duplicate in Automations.** Beside Delete, while nothing is unsaved. Rename… shows the name under Details; Save moves it to the new name (its earlier runs keep the old one, and the notice names any automation that runs after it). Duplicate opens a filled-in copy named "<name>-copy", written on Create. A copy or a rename of a script that waits to be allowed still waits.
- **Desktop notifications in Settings.** Settings > Features has a Desktop notifications switch under the bell's (the group is called Notifications). It does what `herdr-ws-notify --off` / `--on` does, and says what's missing for a click to go to Herdr (terminal-notifier on macOS) or for any to show (notify-send on Linux).
- **Secrets from the keyboard.** ↑ ↓ in the search box open the next or previous secret, and Enter opens the first match.
- **Extend an expired secret in one press.** Its red banner has "Extend 24h" (the secret's own length) under Replace value: it saves only a new expiry from now, and anything else you changed stays unsaved.
- **A secret's note can be changed.** An existing secret has the Note box a new one has, and under its scope "Fixed when made" says the scope is chosen once.
- **Open settings.jsonc and the broker's log from Settings.** When `~/.herdr/settings.jsonc` isn't valid JSONC, the red box on each page that says so has "Open the file"; Settings > Secrets' broker error has "Open log". Both open the file in Files.
- **The installer offers Cmd+, for the package's Settings.** It asks once (Ctrl+, off macOS), no unless you say yes, and says what it costs: `keys.json` becomes yours, so Herdr's updates no longer add their new default keys to it. When it installs Herdr too, its next run asks; an existing install is asked the next time you run it. `--settings-key` binds it without asking, and the uninstall gives the key back.
- **The board's Done column folds.** A click on its header makes it a narrow strip with its count, and a click on the strip opens it again; each board remembers it. Open, it shows its first 60 cards, then "Show all 214".
- **Search the board's tasks.** A "Search tasks…" box in the board's header keeps the cards that hold every word you type in their id, title, folder, agent or notes, in Board and in List; Esc or its × empties it.
- **Start a card and stay on the board.** Shift-click Start or Worktree makes the agent's tab without showing it, so several cards can be handed out in a row.
- **The task dialog shows what a card is tied to.** Under its title: its running agent with Go to, its worktree and its pull request.
- **Pull request marks open their pull request.** In Worktrees, a row's pull request chip opens it and its card chip opens the card on the board; on the board's GitHub side, an item's pull request number opens the pull request, not the issue.
- **Worktrees says how old it is.** "Read 4m ago" sits beside Refresh, and shown full size it reads again by itself every 5 minutes while Herdr's window can be seen.
- **Files keeps unsaved work.** While a file has unsaved edits, Files keeps a copy of them, so closing the pane, switching layouts or quitting Herdr doesn't lose them. Open the file again and a strip offers the draft: Restore puts it back in the editor, unsaved; Discard deletes it. Until you choose, the file can't be edited, so neither draft is lost. The copies sit in `~/.config/herdr-app/data/ws-files/drafts/`, in plain text that only you can read, and go when you save or discard (or after 30 days).
- **Find a file by name in Files.** Typing in the filter box lists every file and folder under the folder whose name holds it, closed folders included, with its folder beside it. ↑ ↓ and Enter pick one from the box; it opens and the tree opens down to it.
- **A + in Files' tree.** It opens the folder's menu (New file…, New folder…) under it, and an empty folder says that + or a right-click makes a file.
- **The file tree works from the keyboard.** After a click on a row, ↑ ↓ move, → and ← open and close folders, and Enter opens a file; ↓ in the filter box goes into the rows.
- **A right-click menu on Changes' files.** Open in Files, Copy path and Discard changes…, which asks in the menu, naming the file, then puts that one file back as the last commit has it (a new file goes to the Trash). If anything else would change, it says why in red and discards nothing: a folder at the path or a file where its folder should be, another row that is the same file under another case.
- **Arrow keys in Files' right-click menu and in Changes' list.** In the menu and its Open in list, ↑ ↓ move one lit row, Enter runs it, → and ← open and close Open in; in Changes' file list, ↑ ↓ step through the files with each one's diff. They work where Files or Changes already has the keyboard, since a right-click never takes it from a terminal.
- **Rename… moves a file in Files.** A name with a folder in it ("lib/auth.js") moves the file or folder there, making the folder.
- **Remove worktree has Cancel and Remove buttons.** With uncommitted changes, the first Remove (or Enter) asks again, "Remove and lose 3 changes", and neither a double-click nor a held Enter can answer it; Remove is as wide as its longest words from the start, so Cancel never moves when they change. While several go, Cancel reads "Stop after this one".
- **History's search says what Enter does.** While it has text, the match Enter takes is highlighted, ↑ ↓ pick another, the box ends with "↩ Resume" or "↩ Go to", and an × empties it. The same in its Automations side's search.
- **Folds are kept.** In the Agents list, a folded heading or agent's tree stays folded after Herdr restarts, as pins stay; one whose agent has closed is dropped once Herdr has run a day without it.
- **Go to the agent that needs you.** A click on "· 1 needs you" in the status bar goes to the first agent waiting on you, in whichever workspace it is, and its hover names it as its Agents row does ("Go to migrate in Globex").
- **Put it back after an Antigravity wipe.** The wipe's toast has a Put it back button and stays 10 minutes (or until you close it); the bell keeps the wipe as an update with the same button, and the command as a fallback. With an Antigravity open it says "Close Antigravity first." and waits for another press; when it's done, "Put back “General Greeting”", and a click on that toast or its bell row opens History at the conversation. The wipe toasts are titled alike: "Wiped “…”" (", to finish later"), "Couldn't wipe “…”".
- **An opened conversation in History says more and has buttons.** It starts with its agent, model and preset ("Claude Code · opus-5-5 · implementer") and shows the start of what the agent said last; under it Copy conversation id and Wipe… are buttons, besides the right-click menu, and its "Resume runs" line ends in a Copy that takes the command with a `cd` to its folder first, so it resumes wherever it's pasted. An opened run has Copy report.
- **Wipe several conversations at once.** In History, Cmd-click (Ctrl-click elsewhere) ticks conversations; a bar under the list says how many and offers "Wipe 3…", whose one dialog checks each, says which can go and why the others can't, and wipes those.
- **Automations' conversations keep out of History's list.** A conversation an automation's run recorded waits behind an "Automation runs N" chip, as Folder gone does, and is tagged "automation" once shown.
